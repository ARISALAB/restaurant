How it works in practice

A Friday at your restaurant, with TableReserve

Not a list of features. What actually happens, from the morning to the next day.

11:40

A booking from Instagram, before you open

A party of four taps the link in your bio and books for 9:00 pm. They get a confirmation email straight away. You see the booking on your phone, along with where it came from.

Online bookings
13:15

The phone rings

Type two letters of the name and the system finds the guest from their last visit. You see which times still have room and answer without searching through a notebook.

Phone bookings
18:00

Reminders go out

Everyone booked for 8:00 pm gets an email with your address and phone number, plus a phone notification if they turned it on. Anyone who can't make it cancels with one tap instead of not showing up.

Fewer no-shows
19:10

A cancellation doesn't mean an empty table

9:30 pm was full and three parties had joined the waitlist. When the booking is cancelled, the first one automatically gets an email that a table is free.

Waitlist
20:30

Mr Konstantinou arrives

His profile shows: seventh visit, nut allergy, prefers the terrace. His booking is already at the right table on the floor plan, with tables joined for six.
