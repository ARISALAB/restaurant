What happens automatically

From the booking to the table

You do none of the following. It all happens by itself, for every online booking and every phone booking that has an email.

  1. As soon as they book

    Confirmation, and the booking on their phone

    The guest receives a confirmation email and, with one tap, adds the booking to Google Wallet or their calendar (Google, Apple, Outlook). You get a notification about the new booking.

  2. 2 hours before

    Reminder by email and on the phone

    An email with the time, party size, address, map directions and phone number, plus a notification on the phone screen for guests who turned it on.

  3. If they cannot come

    One-tap cancellation, not a silent absence

    The guest cancels with one tap from the confirmation email, up to 2 hours before. You know straight away that the table is free, instead of holding it for someone who will not come.

  4. As soon as a table frees up

    The waitlist is notified

    Guests who were waiting for that day receive a “Table available” email with a booking button. Whoever books first gets it.

  5. At the end of the evening

    Measure the result

    Mark “Arrived” or “No-show” and see the no-show rate and cancellations in your analytics and in the PDF report.

FAQ

Reminders, cancellations and the waitlist

When does the guest receive the reminder?

Two hours before the booking time. The email contains the time, party size, the address with map directions and the restaurant’s phone number, and asks the guest to let you know if they can no longer come. If the guest allowed it, they also receive a phone notification at the same time.

Does the guest need to download an app?

No. The phone notification, the Google Wallet pass and the calendar entry are all added from the booking confirmation page, with one tap.

Is there a charge per reminder?

No. Emails, phone notifications, Google Wallet and calendar entries are included in the subscription, however many bookings you get.

Until when can the guest cancel?

Guests cancel by themselves, with one tap from the confirmation email, up to 2 hours before. You are notified immediately and the cancellation is recorded in your analytics, so you can see how many bookings are cancelled and when.

What happens when a booking is cancelled?

Guests who joined the waitlist for that day automatically receive an email that a table is available, with a button to book. Whoever books first gets the table, without you having to call anyone.

Do phone bookings get a reminder too?

Yes, if you enter the guest’s email when adding the booking. Online bookings always have an email, so all of them get the reminder.

In which language are the messages sent?

In the language the guest booked in: English, Greek, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Turkish or Portuguese. So tourists understand what you are telling them too.

How do I know whether no-shows have gone down?

At the end of the evening, mark each booking “Arrived” or “No-show”. Your analytics show the no-show rate and cancellations per period, and you can download them as a PDF report.
