FAQ

About languages and tourists

How does the page know which language to open in?

From the language of the guest’s phone or computer. A German guest sees it in German, an Italian guest in Italian. If their language is not one of the 8, it opens in English, and guests can always change the language from the menu.

Do emails also arrive in the guest’s language?

Yes. The confirmation, the reminder 2 hours before, the waitlist email and the review request arrive in the language the guest booked in. The same goes for the Google Wallet pass.

Is my restaurant’s description translated?

Not automatically. Your name, description and opening hours appear as you wrote them. If you get many tourists, write your description in your local language and in English.

Can I use the admin panel in another language?

Yes. The admin panel is available in the same 8 languages, so staff who do not speak the local language can use it too.

Does it work for a restaurant outside Greece?

Yes. Set your restaurant’s time zone and all times, reminders and emails are calculated in its local time.

Can the booking button on my website use a specific language?

Yes. If your website has pages in several languages, you can put the form in the matching language on each page with a small setting in the code. Otherwise it opens automatically in the visitor’s language.
